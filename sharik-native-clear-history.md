# sharik-native: clearing the history

History items could only be removed one at a time through the row's context
menu, which was not discoverable. The window's bottom bar now has a
*Clear History…* control on the left (it appears only when there is
something to clear) that asks for confirmation before removing every item;
the per-item *Remove from History* stays in the right-click menu. Clearing
history never deletes the shared files themselves — the history only stores
their paths.
