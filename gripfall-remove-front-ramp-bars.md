# Gripfall: remove the front ramp bars

Two tall support bars crossed the open space in front of the hooked ramp, obscuring its silhouette when the 3D camera rotated. They came from the ground-support loop attaching legs to the overhanging lip.

Ground legs are now created only where their ramp attachment is behind the ramp foot. This removes the two front bars and their diagonal braces while retaining the steel truss and supports behind the road. The committed Three.js bundle was rebuilt.

A Chrome screenshot confirmed the opening is clear. The 3D scene rendered without JavaScript errors, and a complete demo still landed successfully. Physics and player controls were unchanged.
